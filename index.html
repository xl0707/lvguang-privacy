履光隐私政策

简要说明

履光无需创建开发者账号。档案默认只保存在你的设备上；只有你主动开启 iCloud 同步后，所选范围才会进入当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私有数据库。隐私保险箱同步需要另行选择并验证身份。当前版本不接入广告、分析或追踪 SDK，不出售个人信息。

1. 适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于 iOS 版“履光”（下称“App”）对数据的处理。隐私问题可通过 su_junyu@outlook.com 联系。Apple 作为设备、App Store、iCloud、CloudKit、系统认证及文件服务的提供方，会依其条款和隐私政策独立处理相关服务数据。

2. App 处理哪些数据

  • 个人档案信息：你填写的姓名或档案称呼、学校或机构、专业或方向、学业或职业阶段。
  • 成长记录：经历的标题、类别、状态、时间、组织、地点、角色、说明、个人贡献、结果、标签，以及证明、成果、技能、目标、要求、提醒、关系、修订记录、网址和其他自由文本。
  • 附件及其元数据：你主动选择导入的文档、图片或其他文件，以及文件名、媒体类型、大小、相对存储路径和用于完整性校验的 SHA-256 值。App 不会读取文件选择器中未选择的文件。
  • 本机设置与同步状态：首次引导完成状态、iCloud 同步开关、保险箱同步选项、保险箱自动锁定时长，以及同步队列、版本、冲突和最近同步时间等运行状态。
  • 系统认证结果：Face ID、Touch ID 或设备密码验证由 iOS 完成。App 只收到验证是否成功，不接收或保存面容、指纹模板或设备密码。
  • 客服通信：只有你主动发送支持邮件时，履光隐私支持及你选择的邮件服务才会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和你主动附加的文件。请勿通过邮件发送身份证件、成绩单或保险箱原始文件。

上述 App 内容仅用于创建、整理、搜索、提醒、展示、导出、恢复、安全保护，以及你主动启用的跨设备同步；不用于广告、营销、数据经纪、跨 App 追踪或建立开发者侧用户画像。

3. 数据保存在哪里

3.1 本机存储

普通档案和附件默认保存在 App 沙箱内。附件使用 iOS 文件保护。履光不运行自建服务器，也不会因你在本机使用 App 而收到这些内容。

3.2 隐私保险箱

保险箱中的本机结构化记录使用设备绑定密钥和 AES-GCM 加密；保险箱附件单独保存并使用 iOS 完全文件保护。保险箱内容不会进入普通搜索、首页、时间轴、提醒、App 内常规导出或 App 内普通备份。保险箱默认不参与 iCloud 同步；如你另行开启该选项，记录和附件会进入当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私有数据库，并可在同一账号的其他设备下载后使用该设备的新本机密钥保存。设备绑定密钥本身不会上传。任何安全措施都无法保证绝对安全，请自行保留重要材料原件。

3.3 可选 iCloud 同步

iCloud 同步默认关闭。你主动开启后，普通档案、普通附件及同步所需元数据会传输到当前 Apple 账号的 CloudKit 私有数据库；保险箱仍默认排除，只有你另行验证身份并开启“同步隐私保险箱”后才会加入。同步用于同一 Apple 账号设备间同步、离线续传和冲突处理。根据 Apple 的 CloudKit 设计，私有数据库默认仅该用户可访问，其内容不在开发者门户中显示，并计入用户的 iCloud 存储空间。履光不会把此同步描述为由本 App 提供的端到端加密。

3.4 导入、导出与网址字段

导入和导出通过 iOS 文件选择器由你发起。导出后的副本由你选择的文件提供商或位置管理，不再受履光的本机访问控制。档案中的网址只作为你输入的记录字段保存；当前版本不会因保存该字段而向目标网站发送请求。

4. 权限与 Apple 系统服务

  • Face ID：用于解锁保险箱，并确认敏感导出、删除或恢复操作；不获取生物特征数据。
  • 文件选择器：仅在你主动导入、恢复或导出时访问你选择的文件或位置。
  • CloudKit 与后台远程通知：仅为可选 iCloud 同步及变更通知服务。App 不发送营销推送，也不以启用同步或系统权限作为使用本地核心功能的条件。

当前版本不申请相机、照片图库、通讯录、定位、麦克风、蓝牙、HealthKit 或 App Tracking Transparency 权限。

5. 共享、服务提供方与跨境处理

当前版本没有第三方 SDK、广告网络、自建服务器或开发者遥测。只有在你开启 iCloud 同步或主动使用系统文件或邮件服务时，相关数据才会由 Apple 或你选择的服务提供方处理。履光不会授权这些服务方将你的档案用于广告或追踪；如未来引入能够访问用户数据的服务方，将要求其提供不低于本政策及 Apple 审核规则要求的保护，并在启用前更新本政策和必要的同意流程。

Apple 或你选择的文件、邮件及网站服务可能根据账号地区、服务设置和其自身政策在不同地区处理数据。详情请查阅相应服务的隐私政策。

6. 追踪、广告、分析与网页技术

当前 App 不使用 IDFA，不请求 ATT 授权，不进行跨 App 或网站追踪，不展示第三方广告，也不集成分析或崩溃上报 SDK。本隐私政策页面为静态页面，不设置由履光控制的 Cookie，也不包含履光添加的网页分析脚本。托管服务仍可能按其政策处理必要的网络日志。

7. 保存、删除与撤回选择

  • 本机记录：保留至你删除。删除记录后，记录会进入“最近删除”并保留 30 天；到期后，App 会在下次运行或回到前台时清理该记录及没有其他引用的附件。
  • iCloud 副本：关闭同步只会停止后续传输，不会自动删除本机或云端已有副本。若要删除云端记录，可在同步开启时删除记录并等待删除及后续清理成功同步；若要删除全部 iCloud 数据，可在 Apple 账号的 iCloud 存储设置中管理履光数据。
  • App 数据：删除 App 会移除其沙箱中的本机内容。受 iOS 行为影响，设备钥匙串中可能保留一个不含档案内容的设备绑定随机密钥；没有已删除的保险箱密文时，该密钥不能还原任何档案。
  • 导出文件：由你在目标文件提供商、App 或接收位置单独删除。
  • 客服通信：在处理问题和满足必要法律义务所需期间保留。你可通过下方邮箱请求删除；法律要求保留的部分除外。

8. 你的选择与权利

你可以在 App 内查看、更正、导出和删除内容，决定是否开启 iCloud 同步，并随时关闭同步。履光没有开发者账号，因此没有需要删除的履光账号。根据适用法律，你还可请求了解、访问、更正或删除履光隐私支持实际持有的个人信息，或撤回相关同意。请发送邮件至 su_junyu@outlook.com；为保护隐私，处理请求前可能需要合理核验请求人与相关通信的关系。

9. 儿童隐私

履光面向一般用户，不以儿童为特定目标，也不会要求儿童创建履光账号或向履光提交档案。监护人应根据所在地法律和未成年人的实际情况协助其管理设备、iCloud 设置及档案。如你认为儿童在支持通信中提供了个人信息，请联系我们处理。

10. 政策变更

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生重大变化，履光会在新版本发布前更新本政策，并通过 App 内页面、版本说明或其他适当方式告知。若新增自建服务器、分析、崩溃上报、广告、第三方 AI、客服上传或其他第三方 SDK，将同步更新 App Store 隐私标签、Privacy Manifest 和必要的授权流程。

11. 联系我们

隐私问题、权利请求或投诉：su_junyu@outlook.com

Lvguang Privacy Policy

Summary

Lvguang does not require a developer account. Records stay on your device by default and only the scope you choose enters your Apple account's private CloudKit database after you enable iCloud sync. Vault sync is a separate authenticated opt-in. The current version contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK and does not sell personal information.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to data handled by the iOS app Lvguang (the “App”). For privacy questions, contact su_junyu@outlook.com. Apple independently processes service data under its own terms and privacy policy as the provider of the device platform, App Store, iCloud, CloudKit, system authentication, and file services.

2. Data handled by the App

  • Profile data: a name or archive label, institution, field of study, and education or career stage that you enter.
  • Archive content: experience titles, categories, status, dates, organizations, locations, roles, descriptions, contributions, results, tags, evidence, outcomes, skills, goals, requirements, reminders, relationships, revisions, URLs, and other free-form text.
  • Files and metadata: files you select, plus filename, media type, size, relative storage path, and SHA-256 integrity value. The App does not read files you did not select.
  • Local settings and sync state: onboarding state, the iCloud sync choice, the separate vault sync choice, vault auto-lock duration, and operational data needed for sync queues, versions, conflicts, and last-sync status.
  • Authentication result: iOS performs Face ID, Touch ID, or device-passcode authentication. The App receives only success or failure, never a biometric template or passcode.
  • Support communications: if you email support, Lvguang privacy support and the mail services you choose receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. Do not email identity documents, transcripts, or original vault files.

The App uses this data only to create, organize, search, remind, display, export, restore, protect, and—if you opt in—sync your archive. It is not used for advertising, marketing, data brokerage, cross-app tracking, or developer-side profiling.

3. Where data is stored

3.1 On-device storage

Ordinary records and files are stored in the App sandbox by default. Attachments use iOS file protection. Lvguang runs no proprietary server and does not receive this content merely because you use the App locally.

3.2 On-device vault

Local vault records are encrypted with AES-GCM and a device-bound key. Vault attachments are stored separately with iOS complete file protection. Vault content is excluded from ordinary search, home and timeline views, reminders, standard in-app exports, and ordinary backups. Vault sync is off by default. If you separately opt in, records and attachments enter your Apple account's private CloudKit database and may be downloaded on another same-account device, where they are stored with that device's new local key. The device-bound key itself is never uploaded. No security measure is absolute; keep original copies of important material.

3.3 Optional iCloud sync

Sync is off by default. If you enable it, ordinary records, ordinary attachments, and required sync metadata are transmitted to your Apple account's private CloudKit database. Vault content remains excluded unless you separately authenticate and enable “Sync Privacy Vault.” Sync supports same-account devices, offline continuation, and conflict handling. Under Apple's CloudKit design, the private database is accessible only to its user by default, is not visible in the developer portal, and counts against that user's iCloud storage. Lvguang does not represent this as app-provided end-to-end encryption.

3.4 Import, export, and URL fields

You initiate imports and exports through iOS file pickers. An exported copy is controlled by the file provider or location you select and is no longer protected by Lvguang's local access controls. A URL in an archive is stored only as a field you entered; the current version does not contact the destination merely because you save that field.

4. Permissions and Apple system services

  • Face ID: unlocks the vault and confirms sensitive export, deletion, or restoration; the App does not obtain biometric data.
  • File pickers: access only files and locations you choose for import, restoration, or export.
  • CloudKit and background remote notifications: support optional iCloud sync and change notifications. The App sends no marketing push notifications and does not require sync or system permissions for its local core features.

The current version does not request Camera, Photo Library, Contacts, Location, Microphone, Bluetooth, HealthKit, or App Tracking Transparency access.

5. Sharing, providers, and international processing

The current version contains no third-party SDK, ad network, proprietary server, or developer telemetry. Data is processed by Apple or a service you choose only when you enable iCloud sync or initiate a system file or email action. Lvguang does not authorize a provider to use your archive for advertising or tracking. If a future provider can access user data, Lvguang will require protection at least equivalent to this policy and Apple's review requirements, and will update this policy and any required consent before enabling it.

Apple and file, mail, or website services you choose may process data in different regions depending on account region, service settings, and their policies. Consult the relevant provider's privacy policy for details.

6. Tracking, advertising, analytics, and web technology

The current App does not use IDFA, request ATT authorization, track people across apps or websites, show third-party ads, or integrate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. This policy is a static page with no Lvguang-controlled cookies or Lvguang-added analytics script. A hosting provider may still process necessary network logs under its policy.

7. Retention, deletion, and withdrawal

  • Local records: remain until you delete them. Recently Deleted retains an item for 30 days. After expiry, the App purges the item and unreferenced files the next time it launches or returns to the foreground.
  • iCloud copies: disabling sync stops future transfer but does not delete existing local or cloud copies. To remove cloud records, delete them while sync is enabled and allow deletion and subsequent cleanup to sync. To remove all of this App's iCloud data, manage Lvguang in your Apple account's iCloud storage settings.
  • App data: deleting the App removes sandbox content. Due to iOS behavior, Keychain may retain a random device-bound key containing no archive content; without deleted vault ciphertext, the key cannot restore an archive.
  • Exports: delete them separately from the destination provider, app, or recipient location.
  • Support messages: remain only as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion at the email below, subject to legally required retention.

8. Your choices and rights

You can view, correct, export, and delete content in the App, decide whether to enable iCloud sync, and disable it at any time. Lvguang has no developer account to delete. Subject to applicable law, you may also ask about, access, correct, or delete personal information actually held by Lvguang privacy support, or withdraw relevant consent. Email su_junyu@outlook.com. Reasonable verification may be required to protect privacy.

9. Children's privacy

Lvguang is a general-audience App and is not directed specifically to children. It does not ask a child to create a Lvguang account or submit archive records to Lvguang. Guardians should help minors manage their device, iCloud settings, and archive as required by local law. Contact us if you believe a child provided personal information in a support communication.

10. Changes

If data handling changes materially, Lvguang will update this policy before releasing the change and provide notice through the App, release notes, or another appropriate method. Adding a proprietary server, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, third-party AI, support upload, or another third-party SDK will also require updated App Store privacy answers, Privacy Manifest, and consent where required.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, rights requests, or complaints: su_junyu@outlook.com
